scifi-ui

Hover treatments for media on a dark page. A particle trace that runs the frame, hairline brackets with readouts pulled from the media itself, a visible play affordance, and orientation aware video. Two files and a wrapper div.

The trace

A short arc of light runs the boundary once, starting from wherever your pointer crossed it, then breaks into particles that decay along a branching tree. Corner brackets push outward and the readouts fade in. Every value shown is real, read off the media element at runtime.

Video, with a play affordance

The native control is small and often lands on top of whatever is burned into the frame, so the frame carries its own. It hides on play and returns on pause, and native controls stay for scrubbing.

Two at once

Each frame owns its canvas and only the hovered one runs, so a page can carry a lot of these without cost.

On a transparent render

The trace sits outside the media and the canvas stays transparent, so it works over artwork with its own alpha without a plate behind it.

Readout panels

The same language as a standalone block. One label, one number, one line of context. This is the whole idea of the repo in one component: say something true, and the ornament stops being ornament.

Put the canvas.trace before the media. The HUD layer and the play badge are created at runtime. Colours come from the tokens on :root in the stylesheet. Read README.md for the reasoning, the tunables, and the compositing traps that cost real time.
